## Description

Your team keeps re-typing ERP data into Word because printed forms don't look like the documents
customers expect. DocStudio fixes that.

Click **Preview & Edit** on any record and it appears as a clean business document: your
letterhead, a "To" box, number and date, a proper items table, bold totals, terms and a signature.
Click any underlined value to change it, then download a Word file that looks exactly like what
you see.

### Why teams choose DocStudio

- **Zero setup, any DocType.** Standard or custom, including your own apps. No print format
  coding. The layout is built from the DocType's own sections, tables and fields.
- **Looks like a real document.** Letterhead, clean tables and clear totals on an A4 page, in one
  of four ready-made themes (Business, Formal, Modern, Simple) or your own fonts and colours.
- **Edit in place.** Fix a rate, add a line, reword a clause, right on the page. Totals update
  as you type. Undo and redo for the whole session.
- **Download as Word.** A true `.docx` built from the same content as the preview, so what you
  see is what you send. Tamil and other Indic scripts render correctly.
- **Ready-made layouts.** Quotation, Sales Invoice (GST), Purchase Order, Delivery Note and Job
  Offer switch on automatically when ERPNext or HRMS is installed.

### More

- One Save button: write changes back to the record, or keep them with the document only.
  Submitted records are never changed.
- Formatting toolbar: size, bold, italic, underline, alignment, colour; format a whole table
  column at once.
- Reusable Terms Blocks, like "Electrical work terms", dropped in with one click.
- Reorder, hide and page-break sections; admins can save the layout for everyone.
- Respects Frappe permissions everywhere.
