# Support

## Getting help

- **Bug reports and feature requests:** open an issue at
  <https://github.com/Inderajeet/docstudio/issues>.
- **Email:** **cipstudioz@gmail.com**. We usually reply within 1–2 working days.
- **Documentation:** see the [User Guide](USER_GUIDE.md).

## When reporting a problem, please include

1. Your Frappe version (and ERPNext / HRMS versions if installed).
2. The DocType where it happens (for example, Sales Invoice).
3. What you did, what you expected, and what happened instead.
4. A screenshot, and any error message (from the browser, or **Error Log** in your site).

Please don't send confidential business data. A screenshot or a sample record is usually
enough.

## Custom layouts

Need a document in your company's exact format? Email us at **cipstudioz@gmail.com** with a
sample of the document you use today.
